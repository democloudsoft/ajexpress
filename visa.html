Documentation & Filing

Experienced Visa Guidance in Karachi

Obtaining a travel or tourist visa requires thorough preparation, accurate paperwork, and compliance with foreign consulate guidelines. At AJ Express, we assist Pakistani travelers in understanding the specific requirements for their destination country.

Our Visa Consultation Services Include:

  • E-Visa Processing: Fast facilitation for UAE (Dubai 30/60 days), Saudi Arabia, Malaysia, Turkey, and Thailand e-visas.
  • Embassy File Preparation: Document checklist structuring, cover letter drafting, flight itinerary reservation, and verified hotel booking vouchers.
  • Appointment Scheduling: Guidance on booking biometric and interview appointments at VFS Global, Gerry's, and embassy centers.
  • Transparent Advice: Honest assessment of document eligibility before submission.

Important Regulatory Notice

Please note that visa issuance and decisions remain strictly at the sole discretion of the respective embassy or immigration authority. AJ Express provides documentation consultation and facilitation services.

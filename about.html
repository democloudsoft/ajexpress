Who We Are

Travel Smart With Experience

AJ Express is a Karachi-based travel agency and consultancy established in 2018. Headquartered at Qurtuba Mall in Bahadurabad, we assist individual travelers, families, corporate clients, and religious pilgrims with comprehensive travel planning.

Our core focus is providing honest, practical guidance for flight reservations, visa application support, hotel accommodations, holiday packages, and sacred Umrah journeys. We believe in clear communication and transparent travel advice so you can make informed decisions.

Sacred Pilgrimage

Complete Umrah Arrangements from Karachi

Performing Umrah is a blessed spiritual milestone. AJ Express coordinates all aspects of your pilgrimage from our Karachi office to ensure comfort, peace of mind, and proximity to the Holy Mosques in Makkah and Madinah.

Umrah Package Components:

  • Hotels in Makkah: Verified properties in the Clock Tower complex, Ibrahim Khalil Road, Ajyad, and shuttle-service hotels.
  • Hotels in Madinah: Front-row and walking-distance accommodations in the Northern and Central Markaziyah zones near Masjid an-Nabawi.
  • Airline Ticketing: Direct flights on Saudia and PIA, or convenient transit connections via Emirates, Qatar Airways, and Flydubai.
  • Ground Transport: Private GMC, coaster, or high-speed Haramain High-Speed Railway ticket bookings between Jeddah, Makkah, and Madinah.
  • Umrah Visa Processing: Electronic Umrah visas with valid Saudi medical insurance included.

Important Umrah Information

Key requirements to keep in mind when preparing for your journey to the Two Holy Mosques.

Passport Validity

Your Pakistani passport must have a minimum validity of 6 months from the date of entry into Saudi Arabia.

Flexible Travel Dates

Packages are available year-round including Rabi-ul-Awwal, Rajab, Shaban, and the holy month of Ramadan.

Ziyarat Excursions

Guided historical ziyarat visits to Ghar-e-Hira, Jabal-e-Noor, Masjid Quba, Mount Uhud, and the Seven Mosques.

On-Ground Support

Dedicated local contact numbers in Saudi Arabia for smooth check-ins and emergency travel coordination.

Air Travel Desk

Domestic & International Airline Ticketing

AJ Express provides complete flight reservation services from Karachi and other major Pakistani airports to destinations across the globe. Our ticketing specialists help you find convenient flight times, compare airline fares, and select the right cabin class.

Why Book Flights with AJ Express?

  • Major Airline Access: Booking support for Emirates, Qatar Airways, Saudia, Flydubai, Turkish Airlines, PIA, Airblue, Fly Jinnah, and SereneAir.
  • Transit & Baggage Clarity: Guidance on layover durations, baggage limits (pieces vs. kilograms), and transit visa requirements.
  • Flexibility & Changes: Professional support for ticket date modifications, flight reissues, and cancellations according to airline fare rules.
  • Group & Corporate Fares: Competitive rates for family groups, corporate delegations, and wedding parties.

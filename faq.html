You can submit your travel itinerary using our online booking form on the website, contact us via phone/WhatsApp at 0332 9999047, or visit our office. We check real-time airline reservation systems (GDS) for the most economical fares and suitable baggage allowances.
To apply for an Umrah visa and package, we require:
  • Original or scanned copy of a valid Pakistani passport (with at least 6 months validity from travel date).
  • Passport-sized photograph with white background.
  • Valid CNIC / B-Form for minors.
No honest agency can guarantee visa approval, as visa decisions rest entirely with the respective embassy or immigration authority. AJ Express provides expert consultation, thorough file scrutiny, valid flight reservations, and verified hotel booking vouchers to maximize approval chances.
We accept official bank transfers / online RTGS/IBFT payments into our agency business bank accounts, cross-cheques, credit/debit cards at our office, and cash payments with official printed receipts.
Date changes and cancellations are governed by the specific fare rules of the airline and hotel booked. Our team handles all reissue and refund requests with the airlines directly to ensure you receive your eligible refunds or date modifications with minimum disruption.
